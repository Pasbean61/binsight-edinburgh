The problem

Fixed timetables meet waste that never arrives on schedule

Edinburgh is a city of tenements and flats. Dozens of households share each on-street communal bin, and how much goes in changes every day. A timetable can’t see short-term lets, students moving out, the festivals or a busy Saturday, so it forces a bad choice: collect too often and waste money, or too rarely and live with overflow.

How it works

Sense, predict, optimise, collect

BinSight adds sensors and software to the bins and trucks a city already owns. We don’t make bins or run trucks. Customers keep their vehicles and crews; we tell them where to go.

Product

BinSight Sensor

A small fill-level sensor that fits communal, trade and campus bins. No bin replacement needed.

Product

BinSight Platform

Live fill map, fill-time forecasts, optimised daily routes, reports, and an API into the route-planning tools councils already use.

Product

BinSight Resident App

Free for residents: report full or misused bins with a photo, earn points for verified reports, spend them at local businesses.

Results

Four ways to run a week of collections

The engine replays test days from the simulated history. Each strategy picks its bins at midnight, the solver routes them, and the bins keep filling hour by hour. We count distance, overflow and wasted trips.

Business model

Three partners, one service

We sell sensors that fit onto existing bins and a platform that turns their data into better collection decisions. The council pays for the sensors and platform, residents add reports, and recycling facilities buy the forecasts. Each partner makes the service more valuable to the others.

Revenue models

Model B: pay from savings

We fit sensors at no upfront cost. The council pays a share of verified savings. Defaults are from our plan.

Sources and method

What is real, what is simulated

Everything about places is real and traceable to an open source. The council does not publish live sensor readings, so fill levels come from a simulation driven by real Edinburgh weather and holidays. We label every simulated number on this site.
